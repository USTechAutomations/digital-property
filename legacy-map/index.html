What you get

  • A checklist page you fill in on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. It makes no network requests.
  • A printable handover folder (print to PDF, or save a copy). For each account you tick: the company's published steps quoted word for word, the date we copied them and the link, the limits the company states, a setting to switch on now where the company offers one, and your own notes.
  • Where your sign-in details are kept, never the details. The page refuses and clears anything that looks like a password, a card number or a wallet recovery phrase.
  • A plain note for crypto you hold yourself, with no recovery phrase in it.

How it works

  1. Open the file. Tick the accounts you use.
  2. For each one, say who should deal with it, where the sign-in details are kept, and what you want done.
  3. Press “Make my folder”. Print it or save a copy for the people who will need it.
